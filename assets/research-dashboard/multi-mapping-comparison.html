Multi-mapping optimization: previous vs. current

16 chiplets × 4 cores · Batch 64 · 8 channels per port · Three A/H/I tile types · Tile rotation enabled · Reflection disabled

This is a comparison of two experiments, not an isolated mapping-only improvement. Previous: earlier mapping pool, no execution-time degradation. Current: 169 published traffic patterns plus available native rotations, up to 0.1% execution-time degradation. Both experiments optimize over multiple mappings. The difference includes mapping-pool changes and the relaxed performance limit. Five current mappings have incomplete rotation archives; only recorded orientations are included. Memory-link PHY capacity is excluded.
