Traffic demand between chiplets

How many MB each chiplet sends to and receives from every other chiplet, on the uniform HW and on the asymmetric HW, each with its own best mapping over all mapping types (native replay, cycles equal the oracle). Both HWs share one shading and bar scale per workload and batch. Each transfer counts once, from the chiplet that produces the data to the chiplet that uses it: bytes a chiplet only passes on are not counted, DRAM reads and writes are left out, and a multicast counts once per receiving chiplet.
