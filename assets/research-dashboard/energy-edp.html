Energy and EDP of the selected mappings

Asymmetric HW with its mapping vs uniform HW with the best of all mappings, for every workload × batch pair of the current selections (CURRENT-c<N>.json). Each mapping is re-simulated by the native binary with its own recipe and the selected HW; only replays whose cycles equal the selection's cycles are used. Updated 2026-10-22 00:08.

  • Speedup = uniform cycles / asymmetric cycles − 1. Energy change = asymmetric energy / uniform energy − 1 (negative = asymmetric uses less). EDP improvement = uniform EDP / asymmetric EDP − 1, EDP = energy × cycles.
  • Energy is Gemini's dynamic energy (MAC, buffers, NoC 0.4 pJ/bit/hop, D2D 2.4 pJ/bit, GDDR6X 10.5 pJ/bit). Leakage is not modelled, so a faster design would gain more with it. The two HWs have the same total link width, so energy differs only through the mapping (hops, DRAM accesses).
