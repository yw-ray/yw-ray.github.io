Template count K: mapping re-search

Does a larger number of chiplet templates K help once mappings are re-searched for its D2D capacities? Every design uses matched ports only (each link's TX modules equal the RX modules at the other end, so no capacity is lost; total module count equals uniform). For each array, the best design that uses the freedom of the larger K (not representable at the base K) was picked with the pooled mappings, and mappings were then searched on it (14 workloads, batches 1/4/16, every non-snake variant of the uniform side, 30 SA rounds, asymmetric side only). All designs are then re-scored on the same grown pools.
