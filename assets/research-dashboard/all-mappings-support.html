DRAM bandwidth is fixed system-wide; SRAM and compute resources scale with total core count. Reflection implementation cost is not modeled; area-exceeded hypothetical mappings are permitted. Native mappings were validated during collection; resized capacities are checked by independent endpoint accounting and schedule replay.
