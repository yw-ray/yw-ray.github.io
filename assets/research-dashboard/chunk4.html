4-chunk forwarding · shared HW and per-workload speedup

Main model from 2026-10-22: a ring-forwarded multicast is pipelined in 4 chunks, so each forwarding step waits for the previous chiplet to receive its chunk (Gemini alone counts link volume only, which makes forwarding free). Uniform is scored under the same model with its best pooled mapping (snake family and split routing excluded on both sides). The shared HW (one HW for 14 workloads × batch 1/4/16) is re-selected under 4 chunks by the group rule (max mean over 42 pairs); total module count equal to uniform. Updated 2026-10-22 12:05.
